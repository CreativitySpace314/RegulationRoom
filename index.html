Pick the behavior first. The diagnosis and support profile come next.

STEP 2 · ADD CONTEXT

Same behavior. Different need.

Diagnosis never tells you everything. It can change what you consider, how much language you use, and what might be underneath the behavior.

ID

Intellectual Disability

Processing time, concrete language, routine, comprehension, communication needs.

SCZ

Schizophrenia / Psychosis

Fear, suspiciousness, internal stimuli, altered perception, need for calm reality-based support.

BPD

Borderline Personality Disorder

Intense emotion, perceived rejection, consistency, validation, boundaries without power struggles.

SMI

SMI / Multiple Diagnoses

Look at current presentation, baseline, triggers, symptoms, coping skills, and environment.

ASD

Autism + Sensory Needs

Transitions, noise, too much language, predictability, space, and sensory load.

TRM

Trauma History

Control, blocked exits, touch, raised voices, shame, threat perception, and safety cues.
