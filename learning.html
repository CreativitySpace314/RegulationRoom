🧠 KNOW THE WHY · LEARNING HUB

Understand more.
Assume less.

This is the learning side of The Regulation Room. Go deeper into diagnoses, experiences and support needs, then connect what you learn back to the person in front of you.

A diagnosis is context, not identity.
🧠

Schizophrenia + Psychosis

Psychosis, hallucinations, delusions, negative symptoms, cognitive changes and what support can look like without arguing about reality.

learn the layers →💗

Borderline Personality Disorder

Emotion intensity, relationships, perceived rejection, identity, impulsivity, stigma and why consistency matters.

go beyond “attention-seeking” →🎧

Autism

Sensory processing, communication differences, routines, transitions, masking, shutdowns, meltdowns and individual preferences.

behavior has context →🧩

Intellectual Disability

Processing, communication, adaptive skills, autonomy, prompting and supporting adults without infantilizing them.

support ≠ take over →🌱

Substance Use Disorders

Alcohol, opioids, stimulants and other substance-use patterns; intoxication and withdrawal concerns; stigma, recovery and harm-reduction thinking.

different substances, different needs →🌿

Trauma

Threat responses, control, proximity, predictability, triggers and how ordinary staff behavior can accidentally add pressure.

ask “what happened?” less literally →🩹

Self-Harm + NSSI

Distress, coping, function, shame, safety response and the important difference between self-harm and suicidal intent.

respond without panic or dismissal →🔀

Co-Occurring + Complex Needs

When mental health, substance use, cognitive disability, autism, trauma and medical needs overlap.

people do not fit one box →🧼

Daily Support + Executive Function

Hygiene, routines, initiation, sequencing, sensory barriers, motivation and preserving dignity during everyday support.

find the barrier →
