✍️ INTERACTIVE PRACTICE ROOM

Documentation.

Write what happened. Show what staff did. Show what happened next.

Good documentation lets the next person understand the moment without guessing. Practice turning loaded, vague shift language into specific documentation that includes the person’s words or behavior, staff support, and the person’s response or outcome.

1 WHAT HAPPENED?→2 WHAT DID STAFF DO?→3 HOW DID THE PERSON RESPOND?→4 WHAT HAPPENED NEXT?
