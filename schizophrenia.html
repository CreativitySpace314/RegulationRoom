🧠 SCHIZOPHRENIA SPECTRUM · EXPLORE, NOTICE, RESPOND

Schizophrenia Spectrum

Schizophrenia is more than psychosis. Perception, meaning, thinking, motivation, expression, sleep, medication experiences and daily functioning can all shift. This page is built to explore one piece at a time instead of turning a person into a checklist.

A diagnosis is context, not identity. Notice the person first.
Related ≠ the same thing.
Schizophrenia↘PSYCHOSIS↗Schizoaffective disorder
Psychosis can occur in schizophrenia and schizoaffective disorder, and it can also occur in other clinical situations. Psychosis is a symptom experience, not another word for schizophrenia.

OPTIONAL · 2-MINUTE AUDITORY-VOICE EXERCISE

Try doing ordinary things while the sound does not politely stop.

This is a stylized, dramatized example of distressing auditory-voice input. It is not a faithful recreation of schizophrenia or of any one person’s hallucinations. Auditory hallucinations can be neutral, comforting, critical, frightening, commanding or something else entirely.

Skip this exercise if simulated voices may be distressing or personally destabilizing for you. Keep the volume comfortable and stop at any time.

Okay. What stuck?

Before looking back up, what was the pretend medication instruction?

Original pretend instruction: Take your morning medication after breakfast.
That was all ordinary stuff. Your name. A color. A food. Three groceries. One simple instruction. If any of it became harder, slower, irritating, easy to lose, or suddenly took more effort, that is the lesson. The tasks did not become more complicated. Your attention had more to filter and carry.

This still cannot reproduce psychosis or schizophrenia. It only offers one controlled, stylized way to notice how persistent auditory input can compete with everyday functioning.
