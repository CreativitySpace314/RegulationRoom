Schizophrenia Spectrum

Schizophrenia is more than psychosis. Hallucinations, delusions and disorganized thinking may be part of the picture, but negative symptoms, cognition, functioning, sleep, medication experiences, mood and baseline matter too.

Don't diagnose the moment. Notice what changed, what the person is experiencing, and what your response adds.

Related ≠ the same thing.

Schizoaffective disorder
Psychosis can occur in schizophrenia and schizoaffective disorder, but it can also occur with some mood disorders, substances or medications, medical conditions and other clinical situations. Psychosis is a symptom experience, not another word for schizophrenia.
