🧠 SCHIZOPHRENIA SPECTRUM · EXPERIENCE + UNDERSTAND

Schizophrenia Spectrum

Schizophrenia is more than psychosis. Perception, meaning, thinking, motivation, expression, sleep, medication experiences and daily functioning can all shift. The goal here is not to “solve” the person. It is to notice how much work it can take to make sense of an ordinary moment.

Empathy first: the room may be neutral while the experience of the room is not.
