OPTIONAL · IMMERSIVE ATTENTION EXPERIENCE

Can you still think while something else keeps competing for your attention?

This is not a schizophrenia simulator. It is a designed attention exercise using recorded voices, uncertainty, ordinary tasks and changing sensory load. The goal is not to say “now I know what schizophrenia feels like.” The goal is to notice what happens to attention, memory, trust and communication when ordinary tasks have to compete with intrusive input.

🎧 Headphones recommendedUse a comfortable volume. You can mute or stop at any time.
↗ You stay in controlSkip it entirely, lower the volume, mute the voices or stop the experience whenever you want.
⚠️ Know yourselfIf simulated voices, paranoia-themed content or sensory distortion could be distressing, skip this activity.

THE TAKEAWAY

You experienced a designed simulation for a few minutes. You did not experience schizophrenia.

Schizophrenia and psychosis vary greatly from person to person. Some people hear voices and some do not. Hallucinations may be neutral, supportive, distressing, familiar, unfamiliar, intermittent or persistent.

The point was to notice what competing experiences can do to attention, memory, communication and trust—and how support can reduce or increase the load.
